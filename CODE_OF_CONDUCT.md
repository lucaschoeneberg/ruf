# Code of Conduct

These original project rules apply in issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces. Everyone is expected to keep collaboration respectful and useful.

## Expectations

- Respect people with different backgrounds, identities, experience and
  communication styles, including newcomers.
- Critique code and ideas with reasons; do not attack people.
- Ask clarifying questions, accept corrections and respect personal boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for other contributors.
- Protect personal information, credentials and private communications.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, repeated
personal attacks and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Report concerns privately using a maintainer's published contact method. Do not
put sensitive incident details or personal information in a public issue. If no
private contact is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private channel
without identifying people or describing the incident.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed;
absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. A maintainer involved in a report
should recuse themselves where another uninvolved maintainer is available. There
is no separate independent moderation team at this stage.

Depending on severity and repetition, maintainers may request a correction, warn,
remove content, lock a discussion, or temporarily or permanently limit participation.
They should explain actions when this does not expose private information or put
someone at risk. Request clarification or reconsideration through the same private
channel. These rules do not change the MIT license or promise a response deadline.
